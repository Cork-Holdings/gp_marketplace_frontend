# GeePay Marketplace product context

## Register

product

## Users

Customers discover SME goods and services, place orders, book appointments, approve quotes, and track work. SME owners and staff manage storefronts, capacity, orders, production, field jobs, and payments. GeePay operators oversee merchants and exceptions. Many users work on mobile devices and have limited time for each task.

## Product Purpose

GeePay Marketplace gives SMEs a practical way to sell goods and services and operate those sales through completion. A single merchant may offer stocked goods, prepared goods, custom production, wholesale, appointments, field service, and labour plus materials. Customers should know what they have committed to, what happens next, and what they owe.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, professional. Preserve the app's established Geepay marketplace visual language, navigation, typography and brand tokens. Use Stripe as an interaction benchmark for clear status, clean hierarchy, explicit next actions, and reliable feedback—not as a replacement visual identity.

## Anti-references

The current workflow pages with dense generic cards, exposed UUIDs, ambiguous status names, and controls that mirror API endpoints. Avoid ornamental effects or motion that slows task completion.

## Design Principles

1. Show the next useful action and the current state together.
2. Use familiar business language; keep internal identifiers and implementation terms out of ordinary tasks.
3. Make costs, timing, capacity, and responsibility explicit before commitment.
4. Keep customer and merchant views consistent while showing only relevant controls.
5. Give immediate, reversible feedback for routine interactions and clear recovery on errors.
6. Ask users only for information the platform cannot already derive from their account, selected business, existing order, or prior choices. Never require a customer to copy a store or user ID.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Support keyboard operation, visible focus, readable contrast, semantic labels, and small screens. Respect reduced-motion preferences while preserving clear state feedback.
